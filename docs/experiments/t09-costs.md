## Retries

Every answer that does not match the schema is stored as `invalid_output` and triggers one retry. In this data: **0** rejected answers (0.0% of the calls that reached the model), $0.00000 spent on them. Calls that failed before an answer (rate limit, timeout) are stored with no tokens, so they add nothing to these figures. Spreading that spend over the valid generations multiplies the cost per generation by **1.000**; the figures below already include it.

## Indicative pricing

**This is an estimate, not a price list.** It answers one question: what would the AI cost be, and what price leaves it at 20.0% of revenue (a 80.0% margin on the AI cost). It uses the blended cost per generation above ($0.00244), so it moves with the photo share, the prompt and the model.

| Products per month | AI cost per month | Price for a 80.0% margin on the AI cost |
| ------------------ | ----------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| 100                | $0.24             | $1.22                                   |
| 1,000              | $2.44             | $12.18                                  |
| 5,000              | $12.18            | $60.88                                  |

With `claude-sonnet-5-5 (effort low)` instead, the AI cost would be about x3.35 (from the comparison above), and so would the price.

What this estimate leaves out, and why the real price must be higher:

- Everything that is not the AI call: hosting, database, storage for photos, the domain, monitoring and your time.
- Payment fees (a percentage plus a fixed amount per charge), taxes, refunds and failed payments. At 100 products a month the price above is about one dollar: a fixed fee per charge would take a large share of it, so the AI is not what limits the price there.
- Free trials and users who generate more than the plan assumes. A real plan needs a usage cap, which `GET /api/usage` already measures per month.
- Prices of the provider change; this table is valid for the prices checked on 2026-10-03.
